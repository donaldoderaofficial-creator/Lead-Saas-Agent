Frequently asked questions

Does Dispatch Pro work with my existing CRM?

Dispatch currently connects to web forms, inboxes, chat, and its API. CRM synchronization depends on the integration configured for your account, so the team confirms the exact connector during setup.

Can I review a reply before it goes out?

Dispatch replies automatically using the criteria you set. You control the rules, qualification thresholds, and access workflow; individual-message approval is not currently presented as a guaranteed feature.

What happens if it misqualifies a lead?

Leads remain visible in the dashboard with their score and status. You can review the record, adjust qualification rules, and update the workflow rather than silently losing the lead.

How fast does it actually respond?

The current product claim is an 11-second median response target with 24/7 coverage. Actual performance depends on provider latency, integrations, and workload.

Is my lead data secure?

Data is protected in transit with HTTPS, responses use security headers, sessions use secure production cookies, and access is protected by authentication, 2FA, rate limits, and audit controls. Dispatch does not claim SOC2 certification here.

T&Cs

How Dispatch Pro’s agents work

Dispatch Pro’s “agents” are software agents, not human employees. They can automatically:

Lead operations

  • Capture and validate leads
  • Score and qualify prospects
  • Route leads through workflows

Engagement

  • Generate replies
  • Track follow-ups
  • Trigger payment and notification workflows

Human review remains important

Agents do not independently perform physical work, negotiate contracts, guarantee sales, or replace human approval for sensitive actions. Human review remains important for payments, custom proposals, misqualified leads, and final business decisions.
