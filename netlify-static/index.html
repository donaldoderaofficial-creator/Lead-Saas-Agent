How it works

Three moves, running in a loop.

01

Connect your sources

Point Dispatch at your web forms, inbox, and chat widget. It starts reading inbound the same hour.

02

Set your criteria

Tell it who counts as qualified — company size, budget signals, urgency. It scores every lead the same way, every time.

03

Let it work the queue

It replies, answers questions, and books the call on your calendar. You show up to a warm conversation, not a cold list.
